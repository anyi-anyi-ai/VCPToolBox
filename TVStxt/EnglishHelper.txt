1. 英语学习助手 (EnglishHelperCore)
插件: EnglishHelper
说明: 英语学习主入口。通过 command 指定子功能。\n\n参数:\n- command (字符串, 必需): 功能指令。\n- text/sentence (字符串, 可选): 句子输入。\n- word (字符串, 可选): 单词输入。\n- grammar (字符串, 可选): 指定语法点。\n- 其他参数按具体 command 传入。\n\n支持的 command:\nanalyze_sentence, sentence_split, lookup_word, grammar_explain, phrase_pattern, rewrite_sentence, quiz_generate, wrongbook_add, review_due_list, review_submit, health_check, reading_aid_v2。\n\n调用示例:\ntool_name=EnglishHelper, command=grammar_explain, grammar=present perfect\ntool_name=EnglishHelper, command=review_submit, item_id=apple, result=记住\ntool_name=EnglishHelper, command=reading_aid_v2, text=She is running in the park every morning., target_level=B2

示例:
tool_name=EnglishHelper, command=grammar_explain, grammar=past perfect
